Tips for Best Results

  • Use Remove tool to delete unwanted background areas
  • Use Flatten tool to smooth color variations and gradients
  • Adjust tolerance for better color matching precision
  • Use Magic Wand to clear one colour everywhere at once
  • Vectorize suits logos and flat graphics; Color output keeps gradients
  • Upscale works best once the background is already clean
  • Higher tolerance removes/flattens more similar colors
